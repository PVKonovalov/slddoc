# Release notes

Earlier history is in the commit messages and in sld-editor's RELEASE.md.

2026-10-30: Source split into directories. The flat package is now internal packages `internal/model`, `svgtree`, `render`, `extract`, `repair` and `cim`, and the root `slddoc` package re-exports their API (`slddoc.go`: type aliases, constants and wrappers), so consumers are unchanged; sld-editor and sld-svg build and pass their tests as before. Each package imports only the ones it builds on (model, svgtree ← render ← extract, repair ← cim). Helpers shared across packages are exported inside `internal/` (e.g. `model.FmtNum`, `repair.SymbolTerminals`, `svgtree.RawNode`); the public API gains only `Point.Add`. Moved to break cycles: `fmtNum` to `model`, `SymbolLibrary` and the layer `<metadata>` writer to `render`, lead inference (`inferLeads`) to `extract`, and tests that call a higher package (render tests that extract, label tests that export CIM) into that package. Added examples: `example_test.go` (godoc examples for Repair, Load/Save, Render, Extract, ExportCIM/ImportCIM) and `examples/xsld2svg`, `examples/svg2xsld` (trimmed sld-svg `svg-sld render`/`extract`) with a synthetic `examples/testdata/feeder.xsld` and `symbols.xml`.

2026-10-30: `EditorSettings.JumpSameVoltage` (`<editor jumpSameVoltage="true">`, `XSLD_FORMAT.md` §3.2): sld-editor's Settings option to put a wire jump also where two buswork wires of the same voltage class cross. A `*bool`: absent means the editor's configured default, so an explicit `false` is kept. Only round-tripped. - **Test:** `TestEditorSettings_JumpSameVoltageRoundTrip`.

2026-10-27: Auto-layout transformer leads keep two rules (`transformers()` and `leadClear` in `cimlayout.go`). First, a lead never crosses or runs along its own node's wires, or itself. Second, it passes over no symbol, and reaches its transformer only in its last segment, from outside. The straight and one-jog shortcuts now take that check, and a lead coming from the far side goes around. `route` treats the lead's own node's wires as solid, and `symbolBox` makes a transformer's box across its axis half its terminal span plus half the reach. A lead with no clear route is drawn anyway and noted in `CIMPage.Warnings` (`cimImporter.layoutWarnings`). Test: `TestAutoLayoutTransformerLeads`.

2026-10-27: Transformer winding voltages (`cimwindings.go`). `CIMSameVoltageTransformers(docs)` lists the transformers whose windings (two or more) all have one voltage, giving each end's mRID, end number, name, kV and `ratedU`. `CIMImportOptions.WindingVoltages` (end mRID → kV) is applied before anything is drawn. The end takes a BaseVoltage of that voltage (the model's own, else a new one). Everything joined to it without passing a transformer moves to a VoltageLevel of that voltage in the same substation, and a line with its own BaseVoltage takes the new one. A side that reaches another winding of the same transformer keeps its voltage, with a warning on the first page. `ImportCIM`'s parsing and indexing moved to `newCIMImporter`. Test: `TestCIMWindingVoltages`.

2026-10-27: Auto-layout spacing from hand-drawn diagrams. Bay pitch is 9 grid steps up to `layoutWideKV` (20 kV) and `layoutWideColumns` (12) above, per band (`layBand.colW`), and the columns of one bay are `layoutLegColumns` (6) apart, from a per-band table of column positions (`layBand.xs`, `columnXs`). `layoutLevel` is 6, was 8. Busbars run 2 steps past their outermost bays. Device names sit on the device's row (baseline +g/2) and are staggered by column only when too long for the gap (`name`). Bay columns are recorded per region (`layRegion.cols`) and moved with it, so `columnX` holds for any pitch after `arrange`. Ring links are checked with `leadClear` and fall back to `route`, then to a warning. A transformer lead whose side run is blocked, with no way around, goes to `route` first. `wireNode` joins the pieces of a node by the nearest way that crosses no device (`clearOfSymbols`). Test: `TestAutoLayoutSpacing`.

2026-10-27: Earthing taps. `earthingChain` finds an earthing branch on a bay's node: a `GroundDisconnector` or `Ground` (with only a ground beyond), or a `Disconnector`/`Switch`/`LoadBreakSwitch`/`Fuse` leading only to one. `cell` keeps such a branch out of the bay's tree (`layCell.taps`). `tapsAt`/`tap` draw it sideways off its node, the first branch right and the next left, with `deviceSide` turning each device. `columnXs` widens the gap to the next bay by the taps' reach (`tapLength`). `leadClear` no longer counts names as obstacles. A lead that keeps the rules is drawn without a warning even when the router found no way. Test: `TestAutoLayoutEarthingTaps`.

2026-10-27: Auto-layout bands in rows (`bandTiers`, `balanceTiers`, `windingVL`). Every band a transformer steps down to goes one row under the band of the transformer's highest winding. A three-winding transformer's middle band, when it feeds no transformer, may move up beside the highest when that shortens the longer row. Bands no transformer reaches go on the lowest row. A row's bands are drawn side by side from the left, with level busbars, their own corridor ranges (`layBand.upOff`/`downOff`) and one transformer row under them; `layBand.xs` starts at the band's x. `goesUp` sends a transformer bay towards its transformer's row (`tierOf`). `placeBlocks` keeps blocks on their drawn row and only pushes them right, clear of what is placed. Bands with no groups are dropped, and a band reserves a transformer row only when a transformer stands under it. `endTransformer`/`inlineTransformer` end a bay with a transformer that reaches the page at one node, falling back to a transformer row when no bay drew it. `layoutError` adds `layoutPageWeight` × (page width + height). Tests: `TestAutoLayoutThreeWindings` (35 and 10 kV share a row).

2026-10-28: Foreign CIM layouts (`cimforeign.go`, `cimimport.go`). `faceConnections` now covers one-terminal devices: `faceAway` turns one whose symbol points at the nearest thing on its node (`nearestOnNode`, which `distToNode` now wraps) to the turn pointing most away from it. `wireNode` prefers a straight drop onto a busbar of the hub (`cimPart.rails`, `dropOnto`) that runs over no symbol, before the nearest way. Before, `cimPart.nearest` checked ports before segments, so a tie went to a neighbour's port. Test: `TestImportCIMPointLayoutFacesAndDrops`.

2026-10-28: No built-in CIM class mapping any more: `CIMClassTable` holds both directions and comes only from the application (sld-editor's `cim` configuration). `NewCIMClassTable(shapes, export, draw)` checks both lists and returns the shapes exported as equipment that the export list leaves out. Removed: `cimEquipmentShapes`, `cimAlternatives`, `builtinLinkCIM`, `CIMClasses`, `ConnectorCIMClasses`.
- `cimClasses` keeps each element class's role and its family of writable CIM classes (`famSwitch`, `famMachine`, `famInjection`, `famShunt`, `famAux`, `famSeries` or its own class).
- Export: a shape with no row, or links to object without a `"28"` row, are not exported (`notExported`).
- Import: `shapeFor(o)` reads the table, with `cimVariant` choosing a `Class.variant` row (condenser, motor, generator, reactor) in place of the hard-coded switch in `element()`. Equipment of a class with no row becomes a `placeholder`: a red Rectangle (shape 3) at its layout position, captioned with its CIM class, no ports, counted in `Report.Skipped` and noted in `CIMPage.Warnings`.
- Tests run with `cimtable_test.go`'s table (`testImport`, `testExport`). New: `TestImportCIMPlaceholder`; `TestCIMClassTable` covers the import rows and the unlisted shapes.

2026-10-28: `CIMHeaders(docs)` reads each document's `md:FullModel` header (`CIMHeader`: profiles, description, modelingAuthoritySet, created); `CIMLayoutHeader` picks the Diagram Layout document's, else the first. `CIMAuthoritySet` exports the authority `ExportCIM` writes. Test: `TestCIMHeaders`.

2026-10-28: Labels turn. `Label.Rotate` (`rotate` attribute, clockwise degrees in (−180, 180], `labelRotation`). `textToLabel` reads a `<text>`'s single `rotate(a[,cx,cy])` (`textRotation`): xsde2svg turns a Modus text about its origin, and the label's anchor moves to where the turn takes it, the angle then being about that anchor. `writeLabel` adds `transform="rotate(a,x,y)"` in both modes. CIM export writes `DiagramObject.rotation` on a turned label's `TextDiagramObject`, and the import reads it. `XSLD_FORMAT.md` §4.6. Test: `TestExtract_RotatedLabel`.

2026-10-28: `parseTwoPortDevice` sets `Mirror` for lopsided shapes (`drawnMirrored`, `mirrorSides`: 29, 33, 37) from the side of the axis their drawing leans to, in the symbol's unrotated frame. `parseVAlign` reads `text-before-edge` (xsde2svg's) as `top`, and `writeLabel`/`writeDigitalDevice` write `top` as `text-before-edge` (was `hanging`, still read). Test: `TestExtract_MirroredArresterAndHangingName`.

2026-10-28: Per-diagram switching-state colors. `Diagram.StateColors` (`<stateColors><state state="0" color="red"/>…`, `DiagramStateColor`) replaces the caller's legend color for each state it lists, in `Render` and `RenderFragments` (`diagramStateLegend`); states it lacks still come from the legend. `SeedStateColors(d, legend)` copies the legend into a diagram that has none. Test: `TestRender_DiagramStateColorsOverrideLegend`.

2026-10-30: Live-state legends for two indicators, for rdss-dms. A FaultPassageIndicator template can use `{fpiStrokeAttr}` (` data-stroke-style="0:c0/,1:c1/,…"`, its ring's stroke per state) and `{fpiFillAttr}` (` data-fill="…"`, its text) built from the FPI legend (`stateColorSet.strokeStyleAttr`), so rdss-dms recolors it from a status point instead of showing the color fixed at save. A PowerflowIndicator's `<text>` carries `data-text="0:→,1:←"` (`powerflowTextLegend`, `powerflowGlyph`'s mapping), so rdss-dms turns the arrow live. The `data-fill` legend is now attribute-escaped. Test: `TestRender_IndicatorLiveStateLegends`. `XSLD_FORMAT.md` element table.
